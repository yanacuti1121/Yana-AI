# Engineering Principles

These principles guide every design decision in Yana AI.

---

## 1. Safety before execution.

## 2. Evidence before confidence.

## 3. Humans remain in control.

## 4. Simplicity beats unnecessary complexity.

## 5. Remove before adding.

## 6. Deterministic behavior is preferred over assumptions.

## 7. Every feature must justify its existence.

## 8. Every failure should improve the system.

## 9. Transparency builds trust.

## 10. Long-term maintainability matters more than short-term convenience.

---

When two ideas conflict,

these principles decide.
