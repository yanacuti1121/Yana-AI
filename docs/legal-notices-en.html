AI Governance & Human Authority

Yana's core axiom is "AI works · You hold authority":

  • Generative outputs proposed by AI models (cloud or local LLMs) may contain hallucinations, errors, or unexpected results.
  • The user retains ultimate authority and responsibility when granting capability approval (ALLOW) for file modifications or shell executions.
  • ActionGate and cryptographic audit logs reduce risk, but do not replace careful human judgment.
🔒

Privacy & Data Security

Yana is architected with a strict Local-First principle:

  • Your source code, API keys, and execution telemetry remain stored locally on your personal machine.
  • When utilizing on-device models (Ollama, LM Studio), your prompts and code stay on your hardware.
  • When choosing to connect external cloud LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini), prompts transmit directly from your client under the respective provider's terms.
