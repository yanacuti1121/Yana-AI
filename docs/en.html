01 — MORE THAN A CHAT WINDOW

Close enough to real work.

Yana brings conversations, projects, files and execution authority into one visible, inspectable workflow. AI may propose an action; Yana distinguishes a proposal from an action that is allowed to run.

Changing models does not change the rules. Cloud or local, fast or deep, the authority boundary stays in the Yana layer.
